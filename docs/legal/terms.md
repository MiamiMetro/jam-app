# Jam Terms of Service

Last updated: 2026-05-14

Jam is a social music app for profiles, posts, comments, direct messages, jam rooms, communities, and audio uploads.

## User Content

You are responsible for the content you post, upload, stream, or send. Do not upload content you do not have rights to use.

## No Tolerance for Abuse

Jam does not tolerate objectionable content or abusive users, including harassment, threats, hate, sexual exploitation, impersonation, spam, illegal activity, or content that violates another person's rights.

Jam may remove content, limit features, suspend accounts, ban accounts, or report illegal activity where appropriate.

## Reporting and Blocking

The app includes controls to report profiles, posts, comments, messages, rooms, and communities. The app also includes controls to block users. Reports are reviewed for safety and policy enforcement.

## Account Deletion

You can delete your account from app settings. Your login is removed and your public profile is anonymized. Some content may remain where needed for conversation integrity, safety records, legal obligations, or abuse prevention.

## Contact

For support, privacy requests, or abuse reports, use the support URL or email configured for the released app.
